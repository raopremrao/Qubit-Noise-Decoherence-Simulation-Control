# Decoherence: T1 and T2

## T1 Relaxation
Energy relaxation time.
Excited state decays to ground state.

$$P(t) = \exp^{-t/T1}$$

## T2 Dephasing
Loss of phase coherence.

$$ρ_{01}(t) = \exp^{-t/T2}$$

Relation:
$$1/T_2 = 1/(2T_1) + 1/T_{\phi}$$

Decoherence is one of the biggest challenges in quantum computing.
